# BPMN with Plone

Documentation on using [BPMN 2.0](https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/) business
process models with [Plone](https://plone.org/).

The diagrams in these pages are rendered from the `.bpmn` sources in this
repository, and most are live: press the play button on a diagram to watch a
token move through the process.
